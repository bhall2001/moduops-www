# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

ModuOps is a free operations planner for model railroad layouts, written and maintained by Robert D. Hall. This policy covers the ModuOps desktop app and this website, moduops.com.

## The short version

ModuOps keeps your layouts on your own computer. There are no accounts, and the app doesn't send your layout data anywhere. When the app starts, it records which operating system it's running on, and nothing else about you. This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## The ModuOps app

Your layouts, modules, trains and everything else you enter are saved in files on your own computer. I never receive them. If you share a layout file, for example by email or by putting it in a shared folder, that's your choice and outside the app.

Each time the app launches, it records which operating system it's running on: macOS, Windows or Linux. That's all that's recorded. I don't store your IP address, your name, your layouts or anything else that could identify you.

To keep itself up to date on macOS and Windows, the app contacts its update server shortly after it starts and every few hours after that. Like any web request, this lets the server see your IP address, the version of ModuOps you're running and your operating system. It's used to deliver the right update and isn't used to identify you. You can turn automatic checks off in Preferences.

## The ModuOps Xchange

The Xchange is a shared collection of modules hosted on Google Drive. If you open it, Google's privacy policy applies to your use of Google Drive. If you contribute a module by email or a GitHub pull request, I'll see whatever you send me, including your email address or GitHub username, and the module may be published in the Xchange for others to use.

## This website

This website is hosted on Amazon Web Services. Their servers keep standard access logs, which include IP addresses and the pages requested, for operating and securing the service. The site itself doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. Downloads are served from Amazon S3.

Links on this site may take you to other sites, such as GitHub or Google Drive. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

## Email

If you email me, I'll use your address only to reply to you. I don't sell it, share it or add it to a mailing list.

## Children

ModuOps isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
